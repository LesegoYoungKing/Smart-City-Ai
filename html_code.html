Smart Meters & IoT Devices

Installation & Connectivity: Smart meters and IoT devices would be installed in homes and businesses to monitor energy consumption. These devices would include smart thermostats, lighting controls, HVAC systems, and EV chargers.

Communication Protocols: These devices would communicate with the app using standardized communication protocols such as Zigbee, Z-Wave, Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth, depending on the device type. They will send real-time data to the app regarding energy usage.

Integration with App: The app would interface with these devices via APIs (Application Programming Interfaces) provided by the device manufacturers. The app would collect and process this data to provide insights and recommendations.

Grid Management

Overview

Effective grid management is essential for ensuring reliable and efficient energy distribution. Our system uses advanced technologies to monitor and control the grid, balancing supply and demand in real-time.

  • Real-Time Monitoring: Continuous tracking of grid performance and energy flow to detect issues early.
  • Automated Control: Automated systems to adjust energy distribution based on real-time data and forecasts.
  • Demand Forecasting: Predicting future energy needs to ensure adequate supply and prevent shortages.
  • Incident Management: Rapid response to grid failures or disruptions to minimize impact.
